Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

You should never have to trade your personal location history just to check the forecast. ZüsCast is built with zero ad trackers, zero data broker sharing, and ephemeral location requests.

Effective Date: November 10, 2026 • Applies to ZüsCast for iOS, Android, and Web (zuscast.com)

1. How Your Location Is Used

Ephemeral GPS

Used strictly to fetch local forecasts, radar imagery, and minute-by-minute precipitation for where you are.

When you grant location access on iOS, Android, or the web, your latitude and longitude are used solely to request live weather telemetry, radar tiles, and city geocoding. Your coordinates are never linked to your personal identity, never stored in a persistent location history trail, and never shared with advertisers.

2. Zero Data Selling or Ad Tracking

0 Trackers

No third-party advertising SDKs, no cross-app tracking, and no selling of personal or location data.

ZüsCast contains zero advertising SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites. We never sell, rent, license, or trade your location or usage data to data brokers, ad networks, or any third party.

3. 100% On-Device Settings & Saved Cities

On-Device Only

Your units, appearance mode, launch preferences, and pinned cities stay locally on your device.

Every preference you configure—including measurement units (°F/°C, mph/km/h/kt, inHg/hPa), Light/Dark Liquid Glass appearance, widget preferences, and saved cities—is stored locally on your device. No account registration or cloud profile is required to use ZüsCast.

4. Anonymous Weather Telemetry Requests

Anonymized

Weather requests transmit only map coordinates—never your name, email, or personal identity.

When ZüsCast retrieves forecasts, Doppler radar tiles, air quality, and lightning telemetry, requests include only geographic coordinates so meteorological services never know who requested the forecast.

5. Optional Push Notifications & Coarse Grid Privacy

Anonymous Push

Severe weather and precipitation alerts use an anonymous push token and coarse ~5 km grid coordinates.

If you opt into weather notifications, ZüsCast registers an anonymous push token alongside coarse coordinates rounded to 0.05° (~5 km) to evaluate severe weather and rain alerts. Alert registrations are updated in-place without retaining historical movement logs, and can be disabled at any time in Settings.

6. Terms of Use & Safety Advisory

Terms of Use

Forecasts and radar are provided for informational use; always follow official government weather warnings.

ZüsCast provides weather forecasts, radar visualizations, and atmospheric telemetry on an "as-is" basis. Because severe weather can develop and change rapidly, never rely solely on any single app for life-safety decisions—always heed official National Weather Service (NWS) or local emergency management warnings and evacuation orders.

Direct Support

Questions about privacy or data handling? Reach us directly at any time.
