Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

You should never have to trade your personal location history just to check the forecast. ZüsCast is built with zero ad trackers, zero data broker sharing, and ephemeral location requests.

Effective Date: November 14, 2026 • Applies to ZüsCast for iOS, Android, and Web (zuscast.com)

1. How Your Location Is Used

Ephemeral GPS

Used strictly to fetch local forecasts, radar imagery, and minute-by-minute precipitation for where you are.

When you grant location access on iOS, Android, or the web, your latitude and longitude are used solely to request live weather telemetry, radar tiles, and city geocoding. Your coordinates are never linked to your personal identity, never stored in a persistent location history trail, and never shared with advertisers.

2. Zero Data Selling or Ad Tracking

0 Trackers

No third-party advertising SDKs, no cross-app tracking, and no selling of personal or location data.

ZüsCast contains zero advertising SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites. We never sell, rent, license, or trade your location or usage data to data brokers, ad networks, or any third party.

3. On-Device Default & Optional Privacy-First Cloud Sync

Optional Sync

No account is required. By default, your units, appearance, and saved cities stay locally on your device.

ZüsCast works completely anonymously without an account. If you choose to sign in with Apple or Google (and optionally link both providers to one unified account), we sync only your sanitized preferences, modular dashboard layout configuration, and saved city pins rounded to ~1 km. We never store your live GPS coordinates, location history, or device push notification tokens in your sync record, and we never store personal contact details or advertising identifiers in our sync tables. You can permanently delete your account and all associated cloud sync records with one tap anytime in Settings > Account (or via zuscast.com/support).

4. Anonymous Weather Telemetry Requests

Anonymized

Weather requests transmit only map coordinates—never your name, email, or personal identity.

When ZüsCast retrieves forecasts, Doppler radar tiles, air quality, and lightning telemetry, requests include only geographic coordinates so meteorological services never know who requested the forecast.

5. Optional Push Notifications & Coarse Grid Privacy

Anonymous Push

Severe weather and precipitation alerts stay strictly per-device and use coarse ~5 km grid coordinates.

If you opt into weather notifications, ZüsCast registers an anonymous per-device push token alongside coarse coordinates rounded to 0.05° (~5 km) to evaluate severe weather and rain alerts. Alert registrations stay strictly per-device, are overwritten in-place whenever your coarse location changes without retaining historical movement logs, and can be disabled at any time in Settings.

6. ZüsCast Pro, Lifetime Licenses & Founder Access

Direct Billing

Direct Apple App Store and Google Play billing with zero third-party payment analytics SDKs.

All purchases and subscriptions are handled directly through Apple App Store or Google Play in-app purchases with zero third-party payment trackers or financial telemetry SDKs. Subscriptions automatically renew unless cancelled in your Apple or Google account settings at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing cycle. Lifetime unlocks and redeemed Founder licenses grant non-expiring Pro access tied securely to your ZüsCast account and can be restored anytime across all your devices.

7. Terms of Use & Safety Advisory

Terms of Use

Forecasts and radar are provided for informational use; always follow official government weather warnings.

ZüsCast provides weather forecasts, radar visualizations, and atmospheric telemetry on an "as-is" basis. Because severe weather can develop and change rapidly, never rely solely on any single app for life-safety decisions—always heed official National Weather Service (NWS) or local emergency management warnings and evacuation orders.
