How do I add ZüsCast widgets to my iPhone Home Screen or Lock Screen?

Long-press an empty area on your iPhone Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, and select ZüsCast. Choose from 12 customizable widgets across Signature, Atmospheric Telemetry, and Outlook Pro in Small (2×2), Medium (2×4), Large (4×4), and Lock Screen sizes. All widgets automatically adapt to iOS Light, Dark, Clear, and Tinted modes.

How do I change temperature, wind, or pressure units?

Tap the Settings (gear icon) in the top-right corner of ZüsCast and open Measurement Units. You can pick a regional preset (US Imperial, Metric, UK Hybrid, or Aviation) or customize Temperature (°F/°C), Wind Speed (mph, km/h, kt, m/s), Barometric Pressure (inHg, hPa), Precipitation, and Visibility individually. Your widgets update automatically.

Why isn't my current GPS location updating?

Ensure Location Services are enabled for ZüsCast under iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → ZüsCast and set to While Using the App. You can also search and pin unlimited cities worldwide from the Search tab without granting GPS access.

How do I customize Light Mode, Dark Mode, or Solar Mode?

Open Settings → Appearance & Theme in ZüsCast to choose between System Auto, Smoked Sky Glass (Light Mode), Storm Navy (Dark Mode), or Dynamic Solar (which automatically transitions between Light and Dark at your local sunrise and sunset).
